# Review on two axes
Report **Standards** (documented repository rules and confirmed bugs) separately from **Spec** (missing, unintended or incorrect requirements). Use a pinned base diff and cite actual changed lines. Do not edit code, approve or merge. Stop after one follow-up re-review in MVP and escalate unresolved important findings.
